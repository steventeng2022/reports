## Detailed findings

### 1. [INFO] /redirect accepts five param names and forwards to /Commerce; canary consumed on second hop (I22)

- **CWE:** CWE-538
- **Detail:** /redirect?{url,u,to,next,return}=canary all 301 to https://www.tripadvisor.com/Commerce?{param}=canary (envoy) - the param is retained but the /Commerce destination is a 404 (15464 B); the canary appears double-encoded in the 404 body and is never followed. No open redirect.

### 2. [LOW] No clickjacking protection on homepage (H4)

- **CWE:** CWE-1023
- **Detail:** No X-Frame-Options on the 200 homepage response; a CSP is present (its frame-ancestors clause could not be re-verified because retests returned the 403 DataDome bot-challenge page without CSP).

### 3. [INFO] Missing Referrer-Policy on homepage (H5)

- **CWE:** CWE-200
- **Detail:** No Referrer-Policy on the homepage response (HSTS 1 year includeSubDomains present).

### 4. [LOW] Two homepage cookies set without HttpOnly flag (C2)

- **CWE:** CWE-1004
- **Detail:** Two of the four cookies set on the homepage lack the HttpOnly flag (three carry Secure).

### 5. [INFO] Homepage intermittently behind DataDome bot wall (S1)

- **CWE:** CWE-916
- **Detail:** The homepage intermittently returns 403 (778 B DataDome challenge via geo.captcha-delivery.com) to non-browser clients; the first probe returned 200 (350 KB).

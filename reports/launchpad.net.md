## Detailed findings

### 1. [INFO] Extra names enumerated from certificate SANs (`D1`)

- **CWE:** CWE-1382
- **Detail:** Certificate for launchpad.net lists 20 name(s) besides the scope host: answers.edge.launchpad.net, answers.launchpad.net, api.launchpad.net, blueprint.launchpad.net, blueprints.edge.launchpad.net, blueprints.launchpad.net, bugs.edge.launchpad.net, bugs.launchpad.net...

### 2. [INFO] Short HSTS max-age (`H2`)

- **CWE:** CWE-319
- **Detail:** HSTS max-age=15552000 (< 1 year): `max-age=15552000`.

### 3. [INFO] HSTS without includeSubDomains (`H2b`)

- **CWE:** CWE-319
- **Detail:** `max-age=15552000` does not cover subdomains.

### 4. [INFO] HSTS not preloaded (`H2c`)

- **CWE:** CWE-319
- **Detail:** `max-age=15552000` lacks the preload directive.

### 5. [INFO] Missing Referrer-Policy (`H5`)

- **CWE:** CWE-200
- **Detail:** No Referrer-Policy header on https://launchpad.net/; full URL (incl. query strings) is sent as referrer by default.

### 6. [INFO] Missing Permissions-Policy (`H7`)

- **CWE:** CWE-200
- **Detail:** No Permissions-Policy header on https://launchpad.net/; browser features (camera, mic, geolocation) unrestricted.

### 7. [INFO] HTTP correctly redirects to HTTPS (`N2`)

- **CWE:** CWE-319
- **Detail:** http://launchpad.net/ -> https://launchpad.net/ (positive check).

### 8. [INFO] robots.txt discloses crawl rules/paths (`R1`)

- **CWE:** CWE-200
- **Detail:** robots.txt on https://launchpad.net/ exposes 5 unique Disallow path(s) (/, /*utouch*, /+loggerhead/, /api/, /ubuntu/+source/utouch*)

### 9. [INFO] No security.txt (no public vulnerability disclosure policy) (`S1`)

- **CWE:** CWE-200
- **Detail:** GET /.well-known/security.txt returned 404 on launchpad.net.

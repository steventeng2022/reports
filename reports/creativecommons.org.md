## Detailed findings

### 1. [LOW] Missing HSTS header (`H1`)

- **CWE:** CWE-319
- **Detail:** No Strict-Transport-Security header on https://creativecommons.org/. Clients may connect over plain HTTP on first visit.

### 2. [LOW] Missing Content-Security-Policy (`H3`)

- **CWE:** CWE-79
- **Detail:** No CSP header on https://creativecommons.org/; no defense-in-depth against XSS/content injection.

### 3. [LOW] Missing X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff (`H4`)

- **CWE:** CWE-693
- **Detail:** No X-Content-Type-Options header on https://creativecommons.org/; browsers may MIME-sniff responses.

### 4. [LOW] No clickjacking protection (X-Frame-Options / frame-ancestors) (`H6`)

- **CWE:** CWE-1021
- **Detail:** No X-Frame-Options and no CSP frame-ancestors on https://creativecommons.org/; page may be rendered in a foreign frame.

### 5. [INFO] Extra names enumerated from certificate SANs (`D1`)

- **CWE:** CWE-1382
- **Detail:** Certificate for creativecommons.org lists 1 name(s) besides the scope host: *.creativecommons.org

### 6. [INFO] Missing Referrer-Policy (`H5`)

- **CWE:** CWE-200
- **Detail:** No Referrer-Policy header on https://creativecommons.org/; full URL (incl. query strings) is sent as referrer by default.

### 7. [INFO] Missing Permissions-Policy (`H7`)

- **CWE:** CWE-200
- **Detail:** No Permissions-Policy header on https://creativecommons.org/; browser features (camera, mic, geolocation) unrestricted.

### 8. [INFO] sitemap.xml discloses URL inventory (`M1`)

- **CWE:** CWE-200
- **Detail:** sitemap.xml on https://creativecommons.org/ lists 19 URLs.

### 9. [INFO] HTTP correctly redirects to HTTPS (`N2`)

- **CWE:** CWE-319
- **Detail:** http://creativecommons.org/ -> https://creativecommons.org/ (positive check).

### 10. [INFO] robots.txt discloses crawl rules/paths (`R1`)

- **CWE:** CWE-200
- **Detail:** robots.txt on https://creativecommons.org/ exposes 1 unique Disallow path(s) (Sitemap:) and 1 sitemap reference(s)

### 11. [INFO] No security.txt (no public vulnerability disclosure policy) (`S1`)

- **CWE:** CWE-200
- **Detail:** GET /.well-known/security.txt returned 404 on creativecommons.org.

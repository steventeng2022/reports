## Detailed findings

### 1. [LOW] /go?url renders the param into a live page (I22)

- **CWE:** CWE-538
- **Detail:** /go?url=canary returns 200 (196 KB) - no redirect; the canary is embedded in the page JSON (queryStringParams.url) on the live homepage. The token is retained in a first-party live document.

### 2. [LOW] /link?url retains the param on the homepage URL (I22)

- **CWE:** CWE-538
- **Detail:** /link?url=canary 301 to https://www.paypal.com/?url=canary - the param is retained on the live homepage URL; the subsequent regional 302 to /sg/home drops it.

### 3. [INFO] /return?url forwards to /webapps/mpp/returns; token consumed on second hop (I22)

- **CWE:** CWE-538
- **Detail:** /return?url=canary 301 to /webapps/mpp/returns?url=canary; the second hop is a 301 to https://www.paypal.com/tw/webapps/mpp/consumer and the param is consumed.

### 4. [INFO] /jump?url forwards to /cgi-bin/webscr jump-page-outside; 404 on second hop (I22)

- **CWE:** CWE-538
- **Detail:** /jump?url=canary 301 to /cgi-bin/webscr?cmd=_jump-page-outside&url=canary; the second hop is 404 (16 KB) - the token is consumed, no open redirect.

### 5. [INFO] Missing Referrer-Policy on homepage (H5)

- **CWE:** CWE-200
- **Detail:** No Referrer-Policy on the homepage response (HSTS with preload, CSP with frame-ancestors and nosniff present).

### 6. [LOW] ssl.paypal.com serves a bare 200 OK (S1)

- **CWE:** CWE-916
- **Detail:** ssl.paypal.com 200 ("200 OK", Varnish) - the ssl alias serves a plain OK page without auth.

### 7. [INFO] checkout.paypal.com 403; shop.paypal.com 503 (S1)

- **CWE:** CWE-916
- **Detail:** checkout.paypal.com 403 (Varnish); shop.paypal.com 503 (awselb) - live subdomains.

### 8. [INFO] sandbox.paypal.com 302 to the regional home (S1)

- **CWE:** CWE-916
- **Detail:** sandbox.paypal.com 302 to /tw/home.

### 9. [INFO] status.paypal.com 301 to third-party paypal-status.com (S1)

- **CWE:** CWE-916
- **Detail:** status.paypal.com 301 to https://paypal-status.com - the status page is hosted on a separate domain.

### 10. [INFO] mail.paypal.com 400; api.paypal.com 404 (S1)

- **CWE:** CWE-916
- **Detail:** mail.paypal.com 400 (3 B); api.paypal.com 404 (Cloudflare).

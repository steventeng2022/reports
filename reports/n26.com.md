## Detailed findings

### 1. [LOW] status.n26.com 302 to a test Statuspage tenant (S1)

- **CWE:** CWE-916
- **Detail:** status.n26.com 302 to https://testsub3.statuspage.io - the production status page points at a test Statuspage subtenant.

### 2. [LOW] app.n26.com 302 to /login (S1)

- **CWE:** CWE-916
- **Detail:** app.n26.com 302 (envoy) to /login - the app subdomain is publicly reachable.

### 3. [INFO] docs.n26.com serves a 108-byte S3 object (S1)

- **CWE:** CWE-916
- **Detail:** docs.n26.com 200 (108 B S3) at the subdomain root.

### 4. [INFO] static.n26.com 403; pay.n26.com 204 (S1)

- **CWE:** CWE-916
- **Detail:** static.n26.com 403 (111 B S3); pay.n26.com 204 (no content).

### 5. [INFO] No HSTS on the www.n26.com alias response (H1)

- **CWE:** CWE-319
- **Detail:** No Strict-Transport-Security on the https://www.n26.com/ 301 (the n26.com apex does serve HSTS with preload; the www alias does not).

### 6. [INFO] Missing Referrer-Policy (H5)

- **CWE:** CWE-200
- **Detail:** No Referrer-Policy on the apex 302 response (a CSP with frame-ancestors and nosniff are present).

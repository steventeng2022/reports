## Detailed findings

### 1. [LOW] Missing X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff (`H4`)

- **CWE:** CWE-693
- **Detail:** No X-Content-Type-Options header on https://bandcamp.com/; browsers may MIME-sniff responses.

### 2. [LOW] No clickjacking protection (X-Frame-Options / frame-ancestors) (`H6`)

- **CWE:** CWE-1021
- **Detail:** No X-Frame-Options and no CSP frame-ancestors on https://bandcamp.com/; page may be rendered in a foreign frame.

### 3. [INFO] Extra names enumerated from certificate SANs (`D1`)

- **CWE:** CWE-1382
- **Detail:** Certificate for bandcamp.com lists 1 name(s) besides the scope host: *.bandcamp.com

### 4. [INFO] HSTS without includeSubDomains (`H2b`)

- **CWE:** CWE-319
- **Detail:** `max-age=63072000` does not cover subdomains.

### 5. [INFO] HSTS not preloaded (`H2c`)

- **CWE:** CWE-319
- **Detail:** `max-age=63072000` lacks the preload directive.

### 6. [INFO] Missing Permissions-Policy (`H7`)

- **CWE:** CWE-200
- **Detail:** No Permissions-Policy header on https://bandcamp.com/; browser features (camera, mic, geolocation) unrestricted.

### 7. [INFO] HTTP correctly redirects to HTTPS (`N2`)

- **CWE:** CWE-319
- **Detail:** http://bandcamp.com/ -> https://bandcamp.com/ (positive check).

### 8. [INFO] robots.txt discloses crawl rules/paths (`R1`)

- **CWE:** CWE-200
- **Detail:** robots.txt on https://bandcamp.com/ exposes 11 unique Disallow path(s) (/, /*_cb$, /api/, /cart/, /checkout)

### 9. [INFO] No security.txt (no public vulnerability disclosure policy) (`S1`)

- **CWE:** CWE-200
- **Detail:** GET /.well-known/security.txt returned 404 on bandcamp.com.

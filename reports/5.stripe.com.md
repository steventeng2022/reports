## Detailed findings

### 1. [INFO] No HSTS on the www.stripe.com alias response (H1)

- **CWE:** CWE-319
- **Detail:** No Strict-Transport-Security on the https://www.stripe.com/ 301 to https://stripe.com (the apex does serve HSTS with a 6-year preload).

### 2. [INFO] Missing Referrer-Policy and CSP on the www alias response (H5)

- **CWE:** CWE-200
- **Detail:** No Referrer-Policy or CSP on the https://www.stripe.com/ 301 response (the apex serves a CSP, X-Frame-Options SAMEORIGIN, Referrer-Policy and nosniff).

### 3. [INFO] status.stripe.com serves the status page (S1)

- **CWE:** CWE-916
- **Detail:** status.stripe.com 200 (892 B Cloudfront, title "Stripe system status") - the status subdomain is publicly reachable.

### 4. [INFO] docs.stripe.com serves 1.3 MB of documentation (S1)

- **CWE:** CWE-916
- **Detail:** docs.stripe.com 200 (1.3 MB nginx) - the documentation subdomain is publicly reachable.

### 5. [INFO] support.stripe.com serves the help centre (S1)

- **CWE:** CWE-916
- **Detail:** support.stripe.com 200 (21 KB, title "Stripe: Help & Support").

### 6. [INFO] media.stripe.com 301 to the S3 support-uploads bucket (S1)

- **CWE:** CWE-916
- **Detail:** media.stripe.com 301 to https://stripe-support-uploads.s3.amazonaws.com/ - the S3 bucket name is exposed in a public redirect.

### 7. [INFO] mail.stripe.com 301 to Google Workspace mail (S1)

- **CWE:** CWE-916
- **Detail:** mail.stripe.com 301 to https://mail.google.com/a/stripe.com.

### 8. [INFO] auth 302 to dashboard; shop 301 to stripe.shop; oauth 404; static 404 (S1)

- **CWE:** CWE-916
- **Detail:** auth.stripe.com 302 to https://dashboard.stripe.com; shop.stripe.com 301 to https://stripe.shop/ (store on a separate domain); oauth.stripe.com 404; static.stripe.com 404.

### 9. [INFO] checkout/pay/billing first-party redirects; edge 301 to apex (S1)

- **CWE:** CWE-916
- **Detail:** checkout.stripe.com 302 to /checkout (CloudFront); pay.stripe.com 302 to /billing; billing.stripe.com 301 to /billing; edge.stripe.com 301 to the apex.

## Reproduction notes

- Scanned 2026-09-30 from Asia/Taipei (UTC+8); single pass per endpoint; parameters taken from live GET URLs discovered on the target (no authenticated sessions). Redirect paths were followed two hops with a canary URL to confirm token consumption.

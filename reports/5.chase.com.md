## Detailed findings

### 1. [INFO] Homepage exposes login-form field names incl. misspelt remember-me (S1)

- **CWE:** CWE-916
- **Detail:** The homepage HTML contains form/field names password, unknwnrememberme (misspelt "unknown remember me"), jp_ltg, cfgCode, CELL, offercode, trk and c - embedded sign-in and offer widgets on the public homepage.

### 2. [LOW] media.chase.com serves the Media Center home (S1)

- **CWE:** CWE-916
- **Detail:** media.chase.com 200 (title "Media Center Home") - the press/media subdomain is publicly reachable.

### 3. [INFO] beta.chase.com 403; static.chase.com 404 (S1)

- **CWE:** CWE-916
- **Detail:** beta.chase.com 403 - a beta hostname publicly resolving; static.chase.com 404.

### 4. [INFO] account 301 to personal.chase.com; secure 302 to auth dashboard (S1)

- **CWE:** CWE-916
- **Detail:** account.chase.com 301 to https://personal.chase.com/; secure.chase.com 302 to https://secure.chase.com/web/auth/dashboard.

### 5. [INFO] Missing Referrer-Policy on homepage (H5)

- **CWE:** CWE-200
- **Detail:** No Referrer-Policy on https://www.chase.com/ (HSTS, CSP and X-Frame-Options SAMEORIGIN present).

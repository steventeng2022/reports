## Detailed findings

### 1. [LOW] secure.expedia.com serves a Checkout page without auth (S1)

- **CWE:** CWE-916
- **Detail:** secure.expedia.com 200 (9793 B, title "Checkout") - the checkout subdomain is publicly reachable without authentication.

### 2. [LOW] One secure.expedia.com cookie set without Secure flag (C1)

- **CWE:** CWE-614
- **Detail:** One of the two cookies set on secure.expedia.com lacks the Secure flag (both carry HttpOnly).

### 3. [LOW] No CSP on the secure.expedia.com checkout page (H2)

- **CWE:** CWE-1021
- **Detail:** No Content-Security-Policy on https://secure.expedia.com/ (HSTS, X-Frame-Options SAMEORIGIN - sent twice - and nosniff present).

### 4. [INFO] Missing Referrer-Policy on secure.expedia.com (H5)

- **CWE:** CWE-200
- **Detail:** No Referrer-Policy on https://secure.expedia.com/.

### 5. [INFO] media.expedia.com S3 root serves an empty body (S1)

- **CWE:** CWE-916
- **Detail:** media.expedia.com 200 (0 B S3) at the bucket root.

### 6. [INFO] Homepage and api rate-limited at the edge (429) (S1)

- **CWE:** CWE-916
- **Detail:** www.expedia.com and api.expedia.com return 429 to the scanner client (edge rate limiting applied).

### 7. [INFO] blog 301 to viewfinder subdomain; auth 404 (S1)

- **CWE:** CWE-916
- **Detail:** blog.expedia.com 301 to https://viewfinder.expedia.com; auth.expedia.com 404.

## Reproduction notes

- Scanned 2026-09-30 from Asia/Taipei (UTC+8); single pass per endpoint; parameters taken from live GET URLs discovered on the target (no authenticated sessions). Redirect paths were followed two hops with a canary URL to confirm token consumption.
